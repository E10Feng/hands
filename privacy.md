# Privacy Policy

Hands ("the app") is a card game for 2 to 5 players that runs inside Apple's Messages app. Effective October 28, 2026.

## What we collect

Nothing. The developer runs no servers and has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code in the app. The app never connects to the internet itself.

## How your games are sent

Each move is sent as an iMessage message to the people in your conversation, through Apple's Messages service, like any other message you send. The message carries the game's state (cards, scores, moves and the player names you type in) in encrypted form, plus an anonymous player identifier that Messages gives the app. That identifier is not your name, phone number, email or contacts, and the app only uses it to tell players apart. Game data, including typed player names, travels only inside the conversation. The developer never receives or sees these messages. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Messages handles them.

## What stays on your device

So games stay in sync, the app saves recent game states on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data. Game messages already in a conversation stay there until you delete them in Messages.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at https://e10feng.github.io/hands/privacy/. For questions, see https://e10feng.github.io/hands/support/.
