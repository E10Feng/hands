# Support

## Getting started

1. Open a conversation in **Messages** with the friends you want to play (2 to 5 players).
2. Tap **+** next to the text field, then **Hands**. If you don't see it, tap **More** and scroll.
3. Pick **Normal** or **Uber**, type your name and tap **New game**. Friends tap the bubble to join.
4. Whoever started the game taps **Deal**.

Everyone needs Hands installed, on an iPhone or iPad with iMessage.

## Common questions

**I can't see my cards.** That's the game. Everyone taps **Ready**, then you get a few seconds to memorize your bottom row. After that your cards stay face down.

**Someone stopped playing.** Tap **•••** on the table, then **Remove** them. Everyone else keeps playing.

**How do I leave or start over?** Tap **•••** on the table, then **Leave game** or **New game**.

**A message says "Update the app to keep playing."** Someone has a newer version. Update Hands from the App Store.

**A message says "This game couldn't be read."** Open the most recent move in the conversation instead.

**What happened earlier?** Tap **History** on the table for the scores and this round's moves.
