Play Hands with friends right in a group chat. Everyone holds face-down cards, gets a few seconds to memorize their bottom row, then plays from memory. Lowest score wins.

<div class="card" markdown="1">
- 2 to 5 players, live in any iMessage conversation
- Normal (4 cards) or Uber (10 cards)
- Peek, spy and blind-trade powers on 7 to Q
- Call Hands when you think you're lowest; everyone else gets one more turn
- Scores add up over rounds until someone goes over 100
</div>

It's just for fun: no purchases, no ads, and no data collected.

Hands is in testing and not on the App Store yet.
